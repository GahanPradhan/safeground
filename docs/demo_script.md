# SafeGround: 3-minute demo video script

**Before recording**
- Open https://safeground-653135417611.asia-south1.run.app once and wait for the map to appear, so the service is awake.
- Use a browser window of 1440×900 or larger.
- Record with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or Loom.
- The narration is about 430 words. Speak calmly.

**Tips**
- The seven places under **Ready to explore** open instantly. A brand-new place takes 1–2 minutes to build, so if you show one, cut the wait in editing.
- AI plans take about 10 seconds. Keep talking while the plan loads.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page (dark) | "Floods in Paiporta, the Beirut port explosion, the Antakya earthquake, the Lahaina wildfire. Disasters hit specific places, and the right action depends on the hazard and the exact spot. SafeGround is hazard readiness and response planning for any place on Earth." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Type *Antakya* in the big search box (show the results), then click the **Antakya** quick start | "Search a city, an address, a landmark or coordinates, or drop a pin. The place is built from OpenStreetMap, Google Earth Engine and disaster records." |
| 0:30–0:55 | **Overview**: hazard profile on the left (*Earthquake — Frequent here*), exposure and the events timeline in the right drawer; click a block on the map | "What hits this place? The profile comes from Antakya's own records: USGS earthquakes, including the magnitude 7.8 in 2023. On the right is who is exposed, with the source under every number. No AI here, just data." |
| 0:55–1:20 | **Readiness** → *Open the assessment* → scorecard → expand fix 1 → *Show blocks on map* | "How ready is it? The scorecard covers people, infrastructure, animals and nature. Each fix shows its impact, evidence and sources, and appears on the map. Code validated every number." |
| 1:20–1:50 | **Simulate** → Hazard **Earthquake** → Moment **Shaking has stopped** → click near the centre → profile **Person outdoors** → **Generate safety plan** → point at the provenance box, then **Directions in Google Maps** | "What should people do? Five steps: hazard, conditions, location, person, plan. The facts come first: danger level, protective move, the nearest open area 331 metres away, with Google Maps directions. Then a plain-language plan from Gemini that passed our validator, every step citing Ready.gov." |
| 1:50–2:20 | Header search → **Lahaina** → Simulate → **Wildfire** → click the fire north-east of town, then the person in town → profile **Person indoors** → Generate | "Lahaina, a wildfire with wind from the north-east. The fire could reach this spot within an hour: leave now, crosswind, never downwind. The schools and elderly homes nearby are listed, because they need help first." |
| 2:20–2:40 | Plan actions: **Share link** (toast), **Print plan** (show the print preview), toggle the light theme | "Every plan can be printed, saved as a PDF, copied, or shared as a link that reopens the same scenario. If the AI fails validation, its text is withheld and the official guidance is shown word for word." |
| 2:40–3:00 | Open **Methodology** from the help menu, then go back to the map | "Code computes every zone, distance and route from official guidance; Gemini only explains. In our test, Gemini alone got the danger level right 30% of the time; with SafeGround, 100%. Built with Gemini on Google ADK, Earth Engine and Cloud Run. SafeGround is for planning and training. In a real emergency, follow the authorities." |
